How it works

From spreadsheet to live listing in three steps

Step 1 · Send

Share your product data

Send us your supplier or PIM export as it is. Excel, CSV or a feed link all work. We check what each marketplace will need and tell you what is missing.

Step 2 · Map

We build each import

We fill each marketplace's template: category mapping, translated titles and descriptions, variants, sizes, colours and images. Then we fix whatever the error report sends back.

Step 3 · Run

Listings stay correct

Prices, discounts and stock are updated on schedule. New products go live in the same format, and you get a monthly summary of what changed.

Services

The whole listing operation, covered

Pick one marketplace or hand us the full set. Each service is priced on its own.

Product import

We fill new-product templates for each marketplace from your export, with EANs, dimensions and images in the right fields.

Category mapping

Your product types are matched to each marketplace's category tree, so the required attributes are complete and listings are approved.

Titles & translation

Titles and descriptions written in German, Polish, Romanian, Portuguese and other languages, following each marketplace's naming rules.

Price & stock updates

Listing reviews

FAQ

Common questions

What does Andronikos Data actually do?

We prepare and maintain your product listings on European marketplaces. That means filling import templates, mapping categories, writing titles in each language, and keeping prices and stock up to date.

Which marketplaces do you support?

Kaufland, Allegro, Galeria, limango, Worten, Vivre, Otto, Bol, Fnac, Zalando and others, including most Mirakl-based marketplaces. If yours is not on the list, ask us.

Do I need a feed tool or integrator?

No. We can work straight from your Excel or CSV export. If you already use a feed tool, we fit into it and take over the catalog side.

How fast can a new marketplace go live?

For a catalog of a few hundred products, the first import is usually ready within one to two weeks of receiving your data, depending on the marketplace's review time.

How is pricing set up?

A monthly fee based on the number of marketplaces and products, plus a one-time setup fee per new marketplace. We send a quote after a short call.

Where are you based?

We are based in North Macedonia and work with brands and distributors across Europe, in English.
